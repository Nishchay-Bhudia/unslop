# Facebook: Before / After

## Before (AI-generated)

> "We are thrilled to announce the launch of our new seasonal menu! Our team
> has worked tirelessly to bring you a truly exceptional dining experience.
> We believe you'll love what we've created. Stop by soon!"

Diagnosed patterns: "thrilled to announce," "worked tirelessly," and "truly
exceptional" are corporate-announcement filler; no specific detail (no dish
named, no date, no number); the ending is a vague invitation, not a real
question — nothing here invites a comment.

## After

Rebuilt using the FLAME structure (Fact/Scene → Line of tension → Ask →
Moment → Emphasis), kept to the 40-80 character-per-line register Facebook
rewards:

> "New winter hours starting Monday. We open at 8, stay open until 8, and
> make coffee all day long. Coming by? Tell us what you're making this
> week."

- **F (Fact/Scene):** "New winter hours starting Monday" — one concrete,
  checkable fact.
- **L (Line of tension):** none needed here — this is an Inform-format post,
  not every FLAME post uses all five beats fully.
- **A (Ask):** "Coming by? Tell us what you're making this week."
- **M (Moment):** "starting Monday" anchors it to a specific point in time.
- **E (Emphasis):** the closing question carries the warmth instead of an
  adjective doing it.

## What changed

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "We are thrilled to announce" | "New winter hours starting Monday" |
| "worked tirelessly to bring you a truly exceptional experience" | (cut entirely — no real content behind the phrase) |
| "Stop by soon!" (no real CTA) | "Coming by? Tell us what you're making this week." (genuine, answerable question) |

**Links:** if there's a menu link, it goes in the first comment, not the
post body — link posts measurably underperform plain text/native posts on
Facebook specifically.

**Hashtags:** none needed, or at most 1-2 — Facebook is not a
hashtag-discovery platform the way Instagram or TikTok are.
